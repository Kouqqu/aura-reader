SIL Open Font License 1.1
-------------------------
The full text of the license is available at https://openfontlicense.org and
ships with the official distribution of each font listed above. In short: the
fonts may be bundled and redistributed with this application as long as the
copyright notice and the license travel with them, they are not sold on their
own, and modified versions do not use the Reserved Font Names.

This section applies to the seven OFL fonts listed before the Zakon entry. It
does not describe zakon_regular.ttf.
